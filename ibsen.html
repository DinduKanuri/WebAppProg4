The Wild Duck

Act 5

Scene - Hjalmar Ekdal's Studio

[Gina takes up the portmanteau.]

GINA.

All right, then I'll put in the shirt and the other things.

[She goes into the sitting-room and shuts the door after her.]

GREGERS.

I never thought it would end like this. Is it really necessary for you to leave house and home?

HJALMAR.

What would you have me do? I was not made to bear unhappiness, Gregers. I must have comfort, and security, and peace about me.

GREGERS.

But can't you have that? Only try it. Now, it seems to me, there is firm ground here to build upon—and begin from the beginning. And remember you've the invention to live for.

HJALMAR.

Don't speak of the invention. Perhaps there's nothing much in it.

GREGERS.

How so?

HJALMAR.

Why, good heavens! What do you really want me to invent? Other men have invented pretty well everything already.

GREGERS.

And you who have made your life's mission of the invention.

HJALMAR.

It was only my poor father's idea.
